For:Rest LIVE's ESG

Powered by Renewable Energy

For:Rest LIVE converts the power used in content production to renewable energy through renewable energy certificates (REC).

We partner with GoodNews Energy to implement a renewable energy structure based on solar power generation, practicing sustainable choices in the music industry.

Environment, Social, Governance

Urban Forests
Through Live

10% of For:Rest LIVE content revenue is donated to urban forest.
We work with the civic organization "FOREST FOR LIFE" to create urban forests and protect the environment.
